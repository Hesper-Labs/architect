# Code of Conduct

This project has adopted the [Contributor Covenant v2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/) as its Code of Conduct.

The full text — including pledges, standards, enforcement responsibilities, scope, enforcement guidelines, and reporting procedures — is published and maintained at:

**https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/**

## Reporting

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported to the project maintainers by opening a confidential issue on the repository, or by contacting the maintainer listed in the repository profile.

All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly. All maintainers are obligated to respect the privacy and security of the reporter of any incident.
